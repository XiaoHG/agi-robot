# Project Roadmap

Status: initial outline only

## Discovery

- Extract proposal requirements.
- Confirm user scenarios and constraints.
- Choose prototype success criteria.

## Prototype

- Select compute, power, sensors, actuators, and enclosure approach.
- Build one end-to-end interaction.
- Establish repeatable build, test, and debug workflows.

## Productization

- Harden safety, reliability, update, diagnostics, and manufacturing documentation.

Do not assign dates until the proposal and prototype scope are confirmed.
